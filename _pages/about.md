<div class="about-bio">
<p>I'm a Master's student in the Language Technologies Institute at Carnegie Mellon and a research assistant in the TEEL Lab, part of the CMU–Accenture Center of Excellence for AI. I work on post-training: SFT, RL, and the question of how little supervision it takes to reliably move a model's behavior.</p>

<p>My recent work is <b>spec learning</b>, which distills preference pairs into a natural-language specification that beats LoRA-based DPO with 50× less data and no weight updates. The spec transfers across models, so alignment becomes something you write down and carry rather than something baked into a particular set of weights. Alongside this I work on persona-conditioned simulation, using latent class analysis over real student responses to build populations of simulated learners that predict item difficulty before a question is ever given to a class.</p>

<p>I'm also working with NVIDIA Research on agentic control. More on that soon :)</p>
